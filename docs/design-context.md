# Design context
Aiden is a personal assistant used by its owner on a Windows PC and remotely from a phone. Core jobs are conversation, voice interaction, PC commands, media, timers, and smart home control. The requested personality is alive, imaginative, polished, and responsive rather than a static dashboard. Preserve animated waves and both chat and voice modes. The phone sends requests to the PC and plays replies locally.

Direction: an intimate listening studio, charcoal olive surfaces, warm amber highlights, Sora display type with Manrope body type. Motion communicates real listening, processing, and speaking states; ambient waves are illustrative rather than an audio amplitude measurement. Mobile is a primary control surface. Respect reduced motion, pause hidden canvases, avoid large animation libraries in the chat shell, and load settings/history on demand.
